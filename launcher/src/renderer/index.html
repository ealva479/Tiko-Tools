Stores

Use "Sign in to stores (Chrome)" in the sidebar to log in once (Google, then each store). Close that window and click a store to sync. Your logins are kept between runs.

Browser extension (optional)

Chrome / Edge: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, Load unpacked, choose the launcher/extension folder. Click its icon and paste the token.
